Starting layouts

Wide top, Grid and Picture top are the layouts the reader starts with. Restoring adds back any that are gone, drawn the way the reader is turned; your other layouts stay as they are.

Weather

The Weather tile shows the forecast for one place. The reader fetches it from Open-Meteo once a day over Wi-Fi.

Search fills these in. Without internet, type them in decimal degrees (south and west are negative); any map app shows them for a place.
