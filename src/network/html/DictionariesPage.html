Where to get dictionaries

Download the StarDict version, then add it above. On the eMinimal hotspot your phone has no internet: download first on your usual network.

  • reader.dict: Wiktionary dictionaries for many languages (one language explained in itself, e.g. English–English).
  • WikDict: free two-language dictionaries from Wiktionary (e.g. German–English).
  • Vuizur/Wiktionary-Dictionaries: Wiktionary from many languages into English, including English–English.
More sources
  • wiktionary_stardict: Wiktionary, updated monthly.
  • FireDict list: freely available StarDict dictionaries.
  • DictInfo: older Wiktionary-based dictionaries.
  • Fictionaries: dictionaries for speculative fiction books and series.
  • World Factbooks Archive: 36 years of the CIA World Factbook.
  • StarDict-Hebrew: Hebrew–English.
